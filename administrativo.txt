* Student ID Card Process: Present your course schedule and proof of address at the ID card office from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
* Graduation Process: Complete social service, obtain a no-debt certificate, and maintain a minimum GPA of 8.0 or an approved thesis.
* ETS Exam Registration (Single-Subject Final Exams): Make the payment at the cashier before the 20th of each month and register the request through the school portal.
* Certificate of Studies: Request it via the institutional portal with digital delivery in 24 business hours.
* Temporary Withdrawal: Submit a form signed by your tutor and career director at School Services before week 8 of the semester.
* Course Schedule Replacement: Go to the school management window with your current enrollment receipt.
* Institutional Scholarship Application: Maintain a minimum GPA of 8.5 and submit proof of family income to the scholarship department during August.
*ITD Admission Process: Online registration at aspirantes.itdurango.edu.mx using a Gmail account, payment at Banorte or Financiera para el Bienestar under agreement CEP 151299, and ticket download via the SIIT portal.
*Certificate of Studies Request: Procedure managed through the School Services department by presenting institutional payment proof and the corresponding form.
*Student ID Card: In-person request at School Services after covering the fee at the cash desk and presenting proof of current enrollment for the school term.
*Facultative Medical Insurance: IMSS affiliation processed through the institute's student services to maintain valid medical coverage during studies.
*Comprehensive Graduation: Completion of study plan credits, fulfillment of professional residency, social service, and selection of an approved graduation option (thesis, monograph, general knowledge exam, or residency).
*Special Exams (ETS): Request and payment at the cash desk according to the dates established in the TecNM school calendar for extraordinary or final opportunity evaluations.
*SIIT Platform Procedures: Access with institutional username and PIN for grade inquiries, academic history, and management of semester re-enrollments.
